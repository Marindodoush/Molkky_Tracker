A free and open-source Android application to track your Mölkky games.
The app automatically manages the turn order, teams (or free-for-all), and applies official rules (score drops to 25 if over 50, turn skipped after 3 consecutive misses).

Features:
- Alias memorization (players).
- Manual or random team creation.
- Session statistics management.
- Translated into 5 languages (FR, EN, DE, ES, FI).
- Color-blind friendly team icons.
- Gender-inclusive text.
